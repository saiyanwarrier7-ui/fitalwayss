Scientific Formulation & Metabolic Methodology

1. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Calculated using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, demonstrated in peer-reviewed clinical literature to be the most accurate predictive formula for normal and overweight individuals:

Male: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age) + 5
Female: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age) - 161

2. Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

Calculated as TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier based on standard Physical Activity Levels (PAL):

  • Sedentary (1.2): Little to no structured physical movement.
  • Lightly Active (1.375): Light daily movement or 1–3 gym sessions/week.
  • Moderately Active (1.55): Active job or 3–5 training sessions/week.
  • Very Active (1.725): Heavy physical occupation or 6–7 intense workouts/week.
  • Extremely Active (1.9): Dual endurance/power training or extreme labor.

3. Body Mass Index (BMI) & Lean Body Mass

BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
Lean Body Mass = Weight × (1 - [Body Fat % / 100])

Note: BMI does not differentiate between skeletal muscle and adipose tissue; body composition values are provided when Body Fat % is entered.

4. Caloric Target Adjustments

  • Fat Loss: Target = TDEE - 500 kcal (moderate 20% deficit, never below 1,200 kcal for females or 1,500 kcal for males).
  • Muscle Gain: Target = TDEE + 250 to 350 kcal (~10–15% lean surplus).
  • Body Recomposition: Target = TDEE - 100 to 0 kcal with elevated protein (2.0–2.4 g/kg).
